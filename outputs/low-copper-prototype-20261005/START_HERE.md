# Osiris PDB REV1.1 Low Copper — test prototype package

Upload **Osiris_PDB_REV1.1_LowCopper_Prototype_Gerbers.zip** for PCB quotation. Use **JLCPCB_BOM.csv** and **JLCPCB_CPL.csv** for assembly quotation.

Select four layers, FR-4, 1.6 mm, **1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner**, ENIG and the requested quantity. Finished outline is 127.5 × 65 mm. Confirm the selected copper combination and assembly/panel requirements with JLCPCB.

This revised board has wider top copper, added bottom power pours, revised In2 ground return and 22 new vias. Its schematic, components, footprints and placement are unchanged. Do not upload the earlier RevA Gerbers with this package.

**TEST PROTOTYPE ONLY:** the lowest standard copper option was selected at the user's request. It is not qualified for 60 A continuous / 100 A for 30 seconds. Estimated copper-loop resistance is about 2.5 times the original 2/2 oz board. See LOW_COPPER_REVIEW.md for the estimate and physical testing still needed.

The BOM contains 56 fitted parts in 41 groups. Exact manufacturer/MPN matching and every polarity/orientation require checking in JLCPCB's assembly preview. F1 is the holder; the removable 7.5 A fuse is supplied separately. R17/R18 are DNP. No order has been placed.
