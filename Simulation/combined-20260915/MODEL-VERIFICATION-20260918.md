## Scope

LTspice 26.0.2 electrical screening of the A-P4 PDB and existing Osiris Rev A behavioral load/power model. This is not hardware qualification or proof that the complete boards work together. No schematic electrical changes were made for this model integration.

- Q1/Q2: Infineon ISC015N06NM5LF2_L1 from the supplied 60 V library, with original parameters and package parasitics. Drain/gate/source connections checked against the live schematic. This version does not simulate self-heating.

- Fresh connectivity check: 59 schematic symbols and both BOMs match the checked release-model connections and values. All nine runner self-tests pass, including a new check that explicitly rejects iteration-limit failures even if a log contains measurements.

## Regression evidence

The first full vendor-model run passed seven of nine active decks; temperature and transient sweeps exceeded the 300-second execution allowance. Preserved evidence: `audit/verification-infineon-first-full.json`.

Those two decks were repeated with a 1200-second allowance. The electrical circuit, accuracy tolerances and acceptance limits were unchanged. Temperature passed in 278.71 seconds. The transient sweep failed after 704.15 seconds with `Simulation Failed: Iteration limit reached`, not a timeout. Evidence: `audit/verification.json`. All sources recorded by the earlier full-run report were hash-checked and remain unchanged: eight of nine active decks now have passing evidence, with transient verification still open.

The transient waveform shows the first 0.2 µH case completed to 0.6 seconds. The second, 2 µH case stopped at approximately 61.76 picoseconds, before the intended power events. Isolated 0.2 µH and 2 µH reruns both passed all original limits in 65.73 and 67.22 seconds. The isolated 20 µH Gear run emitted tolerance-relaxation warnings about 25.84 µs after the 250 ms reconnection event (absolute simulation time approximately 0.2500258424924 s); it was deliberately stopped at 185.67 seconds and rejected. A trapezoidal-integration diagnostic with the same circuit, maximum timestep and tolerances timed out at 180.25 seconds. Neither 20 µH attempt is a pass. Its convergence and electrical stresses still require resolution.

The active list now runs the three harness cases independently: eleven decks represent the original nine test groups, not expanded electrical coverage. Preserved isolated evidence: `audit/verification-infineon-isolated-transients.json`. Ten of eleven independent decks currently have passing evidence; the 20 µH case remains open.

Canonical combined status: `audit/verification-current-vendor.json`, generated by `audit/consolidate_vendor_results.py` from preserved reports after checking every recorded source hash. `audit/verification.json` represents only the most recent invocation, not the combined status.
